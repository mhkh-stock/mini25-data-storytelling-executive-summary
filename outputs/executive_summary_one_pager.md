# Executive Summary — Noor Electronics, Jan–Jun 2026

**SAMPLE / DEMO data only.** Fictional shop, fictional sales.
Never claimed as real client or real business data.

## Headline

Noor Electronics made PKR 8,558,310 net revenue in Jan-Jun 2026; Q2 was PKR 5,164,520, up 52.18% on Q1 (PKR 3,393,790).

## Findings

1. Growth was promo-bought: April-May discounts of PKR 645,700 drove the May peak of PKR 2,020,840, the biggest month of the half.
2. Margin fell from 38.14% in Q1 to 30.53% in Q2 — profit grew only 21.8% while revenue grew 52.18%.
3. Online leads with 41.13% of net revenue; Stationery is just 2.82% and needs a decision.

## Recommendation

Repeat the promo only with a 35% margin floor and a 20% discount cap, and put the next ad spend behind Online.

## Risk

If the promo repeats without a cap, margin stays near 30% — and June already fell 13.71% when the promo ended, so a quiet month costs profit, not just revenue.
